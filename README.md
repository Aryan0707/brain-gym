# B.R.A.I.N. — Brain Gym

A daily trainer for thinking capacity. Watch a curated video, write the one idea in your own
words, rate how well you can explain it — the rating schedules the next review.

- 59 items: 46 YouTube videos + 13 Instagram reels, Hindi and English
- 6 modules: Think Clear · Judge Better · Remember & Focus · Decide & Systems · See Numbers · Speak Sharper
- Installable PWA. All progress stays in your browser's localStorage; nothing is uploaded.
